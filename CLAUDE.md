## STATUS.md (required)

This repo keeps a `STATUS.md` at the root, read by the davegoss.dev dashboard.
At the end of every session — before your final message, and before the last
commit if you are committing — update `STATUS.md` so it reflects reality:

- **Current state** — one or two sentences on what works now and what's in progress.
- **Last change** — today's date (YYYY-MM-DD) and what this session changed.
- **Next step** — the single most important next action, specific enough to start on cold.
- **Blockers** — anything waiting on someone or something else, or "None".

Keep it under ~30 lines; replace old content rather than appending a history.
If `STATUS.md` doesn't exist, create it with exactly those four `##` sections.
Commit it with the session's other changes (don't make a separate commit just for it).
